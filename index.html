About the university

An academic profile grounded in clear pathways.

Moi University exists to give students a structured route through education — from Intermediate foundations, through Degree study, into Post Graduation. The motto is simple and lasting: Foundation of Knowledge.

This site presents the institution’s educational offering, campus presence, and student voices in one place — without noise, and with the information you need to take the next step.

  • Institution: Moi University
  • Pathways: Intermediate, Degree, Post Graduation
  • Campuses: Mumbai, Hyderabad, Delhi
